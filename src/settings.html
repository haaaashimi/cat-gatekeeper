Health Guidelines

The UK Health and Safety Executive (HSE) recommends taking short, frequent breaks rather than longer ones less often. The guideline suggests 5-10 minutes of break time every hour of display screen equipment (DSE) work. This approach helps reduce fatigue, eye strain, and musculoskeletal issues associated with prolonged screen time. These defaults follow this evidence-based guideline.
